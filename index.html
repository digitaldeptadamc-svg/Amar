Botanical Name :

  • Mangifera indica

    • Family Name :

  • Anacardiaceae

    • Local Name :

  • Mango

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi – Aam

  • English – Mango

    • Synonyms :

  • Sahakara

  • Rasala

  • Chuta

  • Atisourabha


    • Habitat :

  • Native to Indian subcontinent

  • Widely cultivated in tropical and subtropical regions

  • Grows well in warm climates with moderate rainfall

  • Grows well in warm climates with moderate rainfall


    • Morphology :

  • Tree: Large evergreen tree (10–40 m height)

  • Leaves: Simple, lanceolate, glossy green

  • Flowers: Small, yellowish-white, in panicles

  • Fruits: Drupe, fleshy, varies in size, sweet when ripe

  • Seeds: Single large seed inside


    • Useful Parts :

  • Fruit (Ripe and unripe)

  • Seed (Kernel)

  • Bark

  • Leaves

  • Flowers


    • Chemical Constituents :

  • Fruits: Sugars (Glucose, Fructose) ,vitamins (A, C), carotenoids

  • Bark: Tannins, mangiferin

  • Leaves: Flavonoids, phenolic compounds

  • Seed: Fatty oils, starch


    • Actions :

  • Deepana (Enhances digestion)

  • Pachana (Digestive)

  • Vrishya (Aphrodisiac)

  • Balya (Strengthening)

  • Rochana (Improves appetite)


    • Properties :

  • Rasa (Taste):

  • Unripe: Amla , Kashaya

  • Ripe: Madhura (Sweet)


  • Guna (Qualities): Guru (Heavy), Snigdha (Unctuous)

  • Vrishya (Aphrodisiac)

  • Virya (Potency): Ushna (Heating)

  • Vipaka (Post-digestive effect): Madhura


    • Indications :

  • Loss of appetite

  • General debility

  • Anemia

  • Digestive disorders

  • Diarrhea (Especially seed and bark)

  • Bleeding disorders

  • Urinary disorders


    • Dose :

  • Fruit: As per diet

  • Seed powder: 3–6 g

  • Bark decoction: 50–100 ml

  • Leaf juice: 10–20 ml


    • Formulations :

  • Amra bija churna

  • Amra bark kwatha
